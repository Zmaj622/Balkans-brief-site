# Balkans-brief-site

Daily brief of the top Balkans news stories for each country for the past 24-hour period:
Kosovo, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Albania, North Macedonia and Croatia.

Published with GitHub Pages. Each brief is added automatically to `_briefs/` by the
"Balkans Morning News Briefing" routine; the front page shows the newest brief and
`/archive/` lists them all. Pages ask search engines not to list the site.

Every Sunday the same routine also copies the weekly organised crime and corruption
round-up to `_ocg_roundups/` (shown at `/ocg-roundup/`) and the weekly hybrid activity
report to `_hybrid_reports/` (shown at `/hybrid/`). Only finished reports are published
here; the logs and data behind them are kept privately.

The briefs are AI-generated summaries of published press reports and may contain
errors; every item links to its sources.
