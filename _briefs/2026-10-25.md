# Balkans brief — 25 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

Polls open today. Items below are from the last 24 hours, mostly 24 October. No results are in yet.

**[Political] Explosive devices thrown at Informer TV building; suspect detained** — Pro-government outlets report two explosive devices ("topovski udar" firecrackers) were thrown at the pro-SNS Informer's Belgrade building on election eve, injuring cameraman Kristijan Mihajlović. RTS reports police detained a suspect, held up to 48 hours on order of the Second Basic Public Prosecutor's Office. Pro-government media call the attacker a "blokader" (a student-protest supporter). The reporting read does not establish a motive, and no critical outlet's account was read. Event date: 24 October 2026.

Sources: [RTS](https://www.rts.rs/vesti/hronika/6069353/napad-tv-informer-zgrada.html) (Public broadcaster; widely accused of favouring SNS) · [Novosti](https://www.novosti.rs/vesti/politika/1663831/snimak-nadzornih-kamera-bacen-topovski-udar-informer-video) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS); state is largest shareholder)

He is held up to 48 hours. The arrest is likely to be read very differently by government and opposition-leaning media. Event date: 24 October 2026.
