# Balkans brief — 17 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Vučić's Novi Sad rally draws a police-reported 110,000, with Fico and Dodik on stage** — The "Ujedinjena Srbija" list held its largest rally of the campaign in Novi Sad, Vučić's first speech there since the 2024 railway-station canopy collapse. He promised justice for the victims and accused unnamed foreign powers of funding the protests. Slovak PM Robert Fico spoke in Serbian and said Slovakia will never recognise Kosovo; Milorad Dodik also appeared. The Interior Ministry (MUP) put attendance at about 110,000 and said there were no incidents. N1 reports the crowd was bussed in, with hundreds of tractors, and that a Razglas News editor was attacked beforehand. Pro-government outlets call it historic. Event date: 16 October 2026.

Sources: [N1](https://n1info.rs/english/news/vucic-at-campaign-event-in-novi-sad/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [N1 – Fico](https://n1info.rs/vesti/fico-u-novom-sadu-srpske-vlasti-vode-politiku-koja-spaja/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Politika](https://www.politika.rs/scc/clanak/794269/mup-na-skupu-u-novom-sadu-bilo-oko-110-000-gradana) (About 50% state-owned) · [Srbija Danas](https://www.sd.rs/vesti/info/poruka-iz-novog-sada-je-jasna-srbi-sa-obe-strane-drine-stoje-zajedno-milorad-dodik-porucio-25-oktobra-za-aleksandra-vucica-za-ujedinjenu-srbiju-video-2026-10-16) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS))
