# Balkans Morning News Briefing — 3 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**Prosecutorial Council fires Special Prosecution chief Blerim Isufaj** — The Kosovo Prosecutorial Council (KPK) dismissed Isufaj in a closed extraordinary session for "mismanagement, incompetence and failure to perform duties", without giving specifics, and named Dren Paca acting chief. The move comes a month after KPK rejected his office's 2025 report; Isufaj had been openly attacked by PM Albin Kurti, and NGOs and the opposition call it political interference (the KPK denies this). Event date: 2 October 2026.

Sources: [Evropa e Lirë (RFE/RL)](https://www.evropaelire.org/a/kpk-shkarkon-blerim-isufajn-kryeprokuror-speciales/33869252.html) (US-government funded) · [Kosovo Online](https://www.kosovo-online.com/vesti/politika/haziri-o-smeni-isufaja-odlucni-borac-protiv-korupcije-danas-je-zaustavljen-2-10-2026) (Biased toward Serbia's ruling party (SNS) / Srpska Lista)

**Government clears PDK's Specialist Chambers amendments; KLA veterans seek further change; PDK demands a sitting** — The government told parliament on 2 October there are no legal obstacles to PDK's bill narrowing the Specialist Chambers' jurisdiction, capping sentences at 15 years and allowing presidential pardons, after the 16 September first-instance convictions of Thaçi, Krasniqi, Veseli and Selimi. On 3 October the KLA War Veterans' Organisation asked for an amendment barring reopening cases already judged by ICTY, UNMIK or EULEX, and PDK sought an urgent session; PDK links the vote to talks on the presidency (Koha headlines report the Kurti–Hamza meeting on 2 October ended without agreement). The Specialist Chambers say they were not consulted; legal experts cite constitutional and EU-commitment limits. Event date: 2–3 October 2026.

## 2. Serbia

**Bipartisan "SERBIA" bill filed in US Senate** — Senators Grassley and Blumenthal introduced a bill identical to the House version: sanctions on individuals undermining democracy and a pause of the US–Serbia strategic dialogue unless free elections and ties to Moscow and Beijing are addressed. It is before the Foreign Relations Committee; five more House members backed the House bill on 2 October. Timing matters ahead of the 25 October election. Event date: 2 October 2026.

## 3. Bosnia and Herzegovina

**Srebrenica Memorial Centre says RS prosecutors ordered a search of its premises** — On 3 October the centre published a 24 September letter from the Bijeljina district prosecutor asking Zvornik police to search it and seize documents; it says dozens of staff are on RS questioning lists and it will not hand over files voluntarily ("they will have to take them by force"). RTRS reported on 2 October that the prosecution had questioned six people so far (via Google News headline only). Event date: 3 October 2026 (letter dated 24 September).

Critics say it breached the silence period and glorified Chetniks; the official framing was commemoration. Event date: 3 October 2026.

## 5. Albania

**SPAK searches Ron Yeffet, Arjeta Puca, Ardi Veliu and Defence Ministry over KAYO arms-industry project** — Special prosecutors searched the home of US-Israeli businessman Ron Yeffet, premises linked to Puca and Veliu, and the Defence Ministry, seizing documents, contracts and devices (Veliu's phone reportedly taken).
