## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Students] Student List names chemistry professor Radomir Saičić as its presidential candidate; Tadić, Dveri, SPO, PSG and others back him** — At a press conference in Belgrade, the movement presented Radomir Saičić (chemistry professor at the University of Belgrade and academician, as reported; no previous political role) as its candidate for the 27 December election. Saičić promised "a sovereign Serbia in which the constitution is respected" and said he would stay within the president's powers and explain his decisions publicly. He accused the caretaker SNS government of still undermining the state. He said he has "a fundamentally positive attitude towards Europe and European values". By evening, Dveri, SPO, Novo lice Srbije, PSG, the Zrenjanin-based PUF, Beograd u pokretu and former president Boris Tadić had all declared support. Event date: 27 November 2026.
