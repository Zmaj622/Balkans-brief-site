# Balkans brief — 23 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Ipsos poll: Vučić list 47.4%, Student List 40%** — Ipsos, in a survey of 3,624 people on 20–22 October, puts "Aleksandar Vučić – Ujedinjena Srbija" at 47.4% and the "Studentska lista" at 40%, with Dačić's list (3.2%) also above the threshold and projected turnout of 64.7%. Danas reports the poll was commissioned by the Ujedinjena Srbija list itself. Event date: 22 October 2026.

Sources: [Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/politika/izbori26/ipsos-rezultati-istrazivanja-izbori/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS))

**[Political] Five held in custody for allegedly planning election-day violence; two have Croatian/Bosnian passports row** — The Higher Court in Belgrade ordered custody for five men (a Croatian citizen, a Bosnian citizen and three Serbian citizens) suspected of planning violence and attacks on 25 October; a Kraljevo court separately ordered 30 days' custody for a man from Vrnjačka Banja. Pro-government tabloids framed two arrested holders of Croatian passports as proof of Croatian meddling; Croatian PM Plenković said holding a Croatian passport does not mean any link to the Croatian state and that Croatia is not interfering. Event date: 22 October 2026.

Sources: [Insajder](https://insajder.net/prenosimo/visi-sud-u-beogradu-odredio-pritvor-petorici-osumnjicenih-za-planiranje-nasilja-25-oktobra) (No clear bias found) · [N1 Hrvatska](https://n1info.hr/vijesti/plenkovic-o-uhicenjima-u-srbiji-to-sto-netko-ima-hrvatsku-putovnicu-ne-znaci-da-ima-veze-s-drzavom/) (No clear bias found)

**[Political] Police search home of Raška student over "SNS bots" database** — Police searched the flat of student Andrej Premović on suspicion of unauthorised access to a protected computer network; he says the pretext is a platform publishing data on SNS-linked bot accounts, denies involvement and calls it intimidation. Seized computers and phones go to forensics. Event date: 22 October 2026.

Sources: [RFE/RL Balkan Service](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/pretres-stana-student-policija-srbija/33881598.html) (US-government funded)

**Closing rallies / silence:** the Student List held its final rally at Slavija (N1, Nova and AP describe hundreds of thousands; police gave no figure) and SNS held rallies in 150+ places; bottle-throwing between supporters and chants of "Mladić" by some SNS supporters were reported by critical outlets in Novi Sad/New Belgrade. The electoral silence began at 00:00 on 23 October (Insajder, headline level). Vučić said on 22 October Serbia will not impose sanctions on Russia and will never recognise Kosovo (Novosti/B92, headline level).

## 1. Kosovo

**[Political] President returns Specialist Chambers law amendments to Assembly** — President Justina Shiroka-Pula sent the amended Law on the Specialist Chambers and Specialist Prosecutor's Office back for reconsideration after a formal request from the PDK, the law's proposer, citing the Chambers' jurisdiction/mandate and defendants' constitutional rights. The move drew sharp criticism from LDK and Vetëvendosje figures and from KLA veterans' organisations, who threatened protests if the law is not fast-tracked. Event date: 22 October 2026.

Sources: [Insajder](https://insajder.net/prenosimo/predsednica-kosova-vratila-skupstini-na-ponovno-razmatranje-zakon-o-specijalizovanim-vecima) (No clear bias found)

**[Political] Kurti accuses Serbia of Russian-style deniable attacks; Macut rejects it** — At the Berlin Process summit near Budva, Kurti cited the Banjska attack and the Ibar–Lepenac canal blast and said Milan Radoičić moves freely under Serbian state protection. Serbia's caretaker PM Đuro Macut called the claims unfounded. Event date: 22 October 2026.
