# Balkans brief — 4 October 2026

**1. Kosovo**

**Kurti meets opposition leaders separately; PDK ties its 22 presidential votes to Specialist Chambers law change** — Prime Minister Albin Kurti met LDK leader Lumir Abdixhiku and then PDK leader Bedri Hamza in parliament, a day after a two-hour Kurti–Hamza meeting ended without a deal. Hamza says the law must pass in both readings before PDK discusses a president; PDK says it would then supply its 22 votes. The government's Justice Ministry said the PDK bill needs specialised review. The constitutional deadline to elect a president is 6 October, after which parliament is dissolved. Event date: 3 October 2026.

**Vučić pledges a 12-minister "government on wheels" at SNS Arena rally** — At the Belgrade Arena launch of the "Aleksandar Vučić – Ujedinjena Srbija" list for the 25 October election, Vučić said he would be prime minister of a government cut from 31 to 12 ministers, and that Serbia will not impose sanctions on Russia.
