# Balkans brief — 19 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Second day of the constitutive session ends again without a speaker; minority parties line up behind Čupić and the vote is expected today** — On 18 November, Radenović, the SNS MP chairing the session, closed it at about 18:00 without a vote. The Student List had proposed sitting until midnight and then withdrew its remaining speakers. Speaker candidate Aleksandar Čupić said Vučić had spoken for almost five hours over two days, roughly the time allotted for the entire debate, and accused the chair of ignoring breaches of the rules of procedure. The Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians (SVM), SDA Sandžak, SPP, the Albanian Coalition and, late in the evening, the Sandžak Democratic Party all said they will vote for Čupić. Tanjug quotes Vučić as saying the debate will end by 13:00 on 19 November, so the vote can take place in the afternoon. Vučić and SNS MPs accused the Student List of falsely presenting Čupić as a full professor when he is an assistant professor, and asked him about "seven flats". Čupić said he will file an asset report with the Anti-Corruption Agency. Pro-SNS Novosti headlines these claims, while critical outlets focus on what they call obstruction. Event date: 18 November 2026.

## 1. Kosovo

**[Political] Convicted ex-KLA spokesman Jakup Krasniqi makes a supervised humanitarian visit home; opposition leaders call his return to The Hague humiliating for Kosovo** — The Specialist Chambers approved a supervised visit to Pristina, the 15th such visit by a detainee. ## 4. Montenegro
