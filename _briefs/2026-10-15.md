# Balkans Brief — 15 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Serbia sends protest note to Croatia over "interference" in the election campaign** — Serbia's Foreign Ministry sent Zagreb a note calling "unacceptable" any attempt at outside influence on the electoral process and criticising what it called one-sided Croatian media coverage linked to regional stability. Croatian outlets framed it as Belgrade objecting to how often Vučić is mentioned in Croatian media; Vučić separately said Serbia "will not pay a racket" to Zagreb or Podgorica. ## 1. Kosovo

**[Military] Italy's Maj. Gen. Roberto Vergori takes over KFOR from Turkey's Özkan Ulutaş** — The handover took place in Pristina on 14 October; Vergori met Prime Minister Kurti, Interior Minister Sveçla and police director Hoxha on security and cooperation, and Kurti repeated Pristina's NATO-membership aim. Italian media cited by Lajmi.net say responsibility for the main Ibar bridge in Mitrovica is already moving to Kosovo Police, which is also in the security set-up at Visoki Dečani, though the command calls the situation "fragile" with potential for new tension in the north; Ulutaş said KFOR "optimisation" is not a transfer of mandate. SACEUR-level NATO officials also met Serb List representatives, who were told security depends on political solutions, not military measures. Event date: 14 October 2026.

**[Military] Italian defence minister Crosetto: Italy's engagement continues even if KFOR shrinks; radars in the Balkans under consideration** — Speaking in Pristina after the ceremony (per ANSA), Guido Crosetto said Italy will keep its security commitment if KFOR reduces its presence and that stationing military radars in the region is being considered to protect the Balkans and Italy against future missile threats.
