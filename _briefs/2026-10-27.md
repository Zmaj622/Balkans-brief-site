**[Political] Crowd storms Informer, egg-throwing at Pink; Student List disowns the raid** — On the evening of 26 October several dozen citizens, including Dijana Hrka, mother of a victim of the Novi Sad canopy collapse, entered TV Informer and confronted editor Dragan J. ## 1. Kosovo

**[Military] Kosovo prosecutors indict Stefan Radulović over Banjska attack** — The Special Prosecution filed an indictment with the Basic Court in Pristina against Stefan Radulović of North Mitrovica for terrorism and serious crimes against the constitutional order, alleging he took part in the 24 September 2023 armed attack on Kosovo Police in Banjska as a member of a "heavily armed terrorist group" aiming to detach the north. He was arrested at Merdare in April and is in detention; an earlier indictment (September 2024) covers 45 people. Event date: 26 October 2026.
