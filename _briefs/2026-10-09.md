# Balkans brief — 9 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**President Shiroka-Pula starts work, meets Prime Minister Kurti** — On her first working day Justina Shiroka-Pula visited Prekaz and the graves of Ibrahim Rugova and Adem Demaçi, then received Albin Kurti at the Presidency. Kurti said Kosovo "finally has functional institutions" after more than a year of blockade, and the Presidency pledged close coordination; the opposition is already debating the Specialist Chambers law amendments she inherits. Event date: 8 October 2026.

Event date: 8 October 2026.

## 5. Albania

**Parliament passes territorial reform with 85 votes amid chaos** — Early on 9 October, after a marathon session, MPs approved the reform cutting municipalities from 61 to 46 (85 for, 34 against); socialists Erion Braçe and Elisa Spiropali, who had criticised it, voted in favour. Democrat group leader Gazment Bardhi threw water at socialist MPs and objects were thrown toward PM Rama; the presence of ex-deputy PM Belinda Balluku, a corruption defendant, enraged the opposition. Pro-government Vizion Plus focuses on Bardhi's behaviour. Event date: 8–9 October 2026.
