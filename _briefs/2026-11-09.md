# Balkans brief — 9 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Brnabić sets 17 November constitutive session and asks Student List for talks on deputy speakers and committees** — Acting President and outgoing Speaker Ana Brnabić scheduled the new Assembly's first sitting for 17 November, 10:00. On TV Pink she said nobody from the Student List had contacted Assembly services, and urged them to open talks "next week" on the number of deputy speakers and which committees the opposition chairs. Student List figures (Baucal, Šoškić) say they are ready and will announce their candidate for Speaker themselves. Event date: 8 November 2026.

## 1. Kosovo

**[Military] Government adopts 2027 draft budget with €224.2m for Defence, €122.4m capital investment** — Of a €4.27bn draft budget, the Defence Ministry (including the Kosovo Security Force) gets €224,168,705, of which €122.4m is capital investment and €58.7m wages, aimed at KSF modernisation. Analysts cite missiles, drones and air defence as priorities. Event date: 8 November 2026.

Sources: Single source — [Lajmi.net](https://lajmi.net/buxheti-per-vitin-2027-224-milione-euro-per-ministrine-e-mbrojtjes-122-milione-per-investime-kapitale/) (No clear bias found)

**[Political] UK prime minister Andy Burnham urges Kosovo to respect the full mandate of the Specialist Chambers** — In a letter to Albin Kurti, Burnham "strongly encouraged" Kosovo to honour the Hague court's full mandate while reaffirming support for Kosovo as a sovereign, multi-ethnic state. It follows October amendments to the Specialist Chambers law that alarmed European partners, after protests over the 16 September verdict against four former KLA leaders. Event date: 8 November 2026.

Sources: [RFE/RL Albanian](https://www.evropaelire.org/a/britania-kosova-gjykata-speciale/33893294.html) (US-government funded) · [Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/politika/premijer-bernam-velika-britanija-ce-nastaviti-podrsku-kosovu/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS))
