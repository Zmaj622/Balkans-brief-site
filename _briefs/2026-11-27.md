# Balkans brief — 27 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Čupić calls the presidential election for 27 December; RIK sets 6 December as the deadline for candidacies** — Speaker and acting president Aleksandar Čupić signed the decision on 26 November, calling the vote after Vučić's September resignation. The Republic Electoral Commission (RIK) adopted the forms and timetable the same day: candidacies are due by 6 December, the draw for ballot order by 11 December, and ballots will be cream-coloured.
