# Balkans brief — 17 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Military] Chief of the General Staff Milan Mojsilović retired "at his own request"; Lt Gen Tiosav Janković takes over as acting chief** — The Defence Ministry said General Milan Mojsilović was retired from 16 November by decree of acting president Ana Brnabić, at age 59 and with more than 42 years of pensionable service. Deputy Chief of the General Staff Lt Gen Tiosav Janković will perform the duty until a new chief is appointed. Mojsilović led the army since 2018 and his service had been extended in July 2025. A former Yugoslav Army officer told RFE/RL that Mojsilović was a Vučić loyalist and that leaving before 2027 suggests "covering up something still to come". Critical Nova says he "allowed the army to become an SNS party organisation". This is the first change at the top of the armed forces since the change of power. Event date: 16 November 2026.
