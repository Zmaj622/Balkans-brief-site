# Balkans brief — 5 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] REM bans Informer TV's "Prve info" for seven days.** — The media regulator's council voted unanimously to ban the programme for a "particularly serious" breach of the law during the election campaign; it is a temporary ban on one programme, not a loss of licence. Acting president Ana Brnabić, SNS leader Miloš Vučević and acting defence minister Bratislav Gašić joined a support rally of roughly 100 people outside; afterwards some participants surrounded, insulted and pushed REM council member Rodoljub Šabić. Informer editor Dragan J. Vučićević threatened lawsuits and international arbitration. SNS-aligned outlets call it censorship by the Student List side; critical outlets stress the legal basis and the pushing of Šabić. Event date: 4 November 2026.

**[OCG/Corruption] SAJ commander Igor Žmirić among nine police held over alleged torture in the Government garage.** — MUP said eight serving officers and one former officer were held for up to 48 hours on suspicion of ill-treatment and torture of several people in the garage of the Government building on the night of 14/15 August 2025; the order came from the First Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Belgrade. Žmirić heads the SAJ special anti-terrorist unit. They are suspects only. Lawyers for the students allege the "protectors" of the perpetrators must also answer. Event date: 4 November 2026.

**[Political] European Commission report: Serbia made no progress on the judiciary; EU ambassador says Student List made commitments.** — The annual report says undue political pressure on the judiciary and prosecution rose significantly.
