## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Prosecution oversight commission accuses Belgrade prosecutors of targeting the student movement; Stefanović hits back with a criminal complaint** — A Supreme Public Prosecutor's Office commission reviewed seven cases from the Belgrade Higher Prosecutor's Office, covering the "sound cannon", Vučić's pardons and attacks on students. It found serious breaches and use of the prosecution for political ends. It says prosecutors Miodrag Marković and Aleksandar Milošević may have abused their office by using the probe into a student's death to collect information on the student movement.
