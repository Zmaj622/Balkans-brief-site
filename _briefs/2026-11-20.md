# Balkans brief — 20 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Čupić elected speaker; Student List takes all six deputy speaker posts and SNS walks out** — On 19 November the Assembly elected Student List candidate Aleksandar Čupić as speaker with 140 votes. SNS candidate Stojan Radenović got 97. After a debate that ran past midnight, MPs elected six deputy speakers, all from the Student List: Jelena Petrović, Lidija Dimitrijević, Marija Bjelobrk, Sara Valentik, Tibor Lazar and Adem Mavrić. They also appointed Mirjana Radaković as secretary general. SNS MPs walked out before the vote. Ana Brnabić said the Student List had "killed 35 years of democracy". Student List caucus leader Jovo Bakić said the list had won among minority voters, which is why two minority MPs are among its deputies. Čupić reprimanded Vučić during the debate. The US embassy and Commissioner Marta Kos congratulated him. The session resumes on 24 November to set up committees. Pro-SNS Novosti headlines "Student List killed democracy in Serbia"; critical outlets call it the "first free institution". Event date: 19 November 2026.
