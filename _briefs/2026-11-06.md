# Balkans brief — 6 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] RIK proclaims final results: Student List 140 seats, Vučić's list 103** — The election commission certified the 25 October vote (with the 1 November repeat votes). The Student List won 2,354,417 votes and 140 of 250 seats, Vučić's United Serbia 103, and minority lists took 7 more. The new Assembly must be constituted within 30 days, and a government elected within 90 days of that; outgoing speaker Ana Brnabić (also acting president) convenes it. A RIK member asked prosecutors to examine suspected vote buying in the repeat voting. Event date: 5 November 2026.

**[Political] Moscow ties gas-deal talks to the new government** — Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said extending the gas contract (expiring 31 December) will be considered once Serbia's new institutions submit an official request. SNS-aligned outlets framed it as Moscow "answering" the students. Event date: 5 November 2026.
