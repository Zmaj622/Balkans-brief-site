# Balkans brief — 18 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] [Students] New National Assembly constituted; Student List holds 140 of 250 seats, and its speaker candidate Aleksandar Čupić faces SNS's Stojan Radenović today** — On the first day of the constitutive session (17 November), 250 mandates were verified: 140 for the Student List, 103 for the SNS-led "Aleksandar Vučić – Serbia Wins" and 7 for minority parties. Two groups were formed: the Student List group, led by Jovo Bakić (a sociology professor at the Belgrade Faculty of Philosophy and a long-time critic of the government), and Vučić's opposition group. The session adjourned without electing a speaker and resumes at 10:00 on 18 November. Čupić (a professor, nominated by students of the Faculty of Transport and Traffic Engineering) faces Radenović, and the new speaker also becomes acting president. Bakić said the deputy speaker posts will go to Student List MPs from national minorities, including Sara Valentik from the Slovak community. Blic notes that the 90-day deadline to form a government now runs to 15 February 2027, and that Dejan Šoškić and Ilija Srdanović are mentioned as possible prime ministers. Speaking for about 1 hour 43 minutes, Vučić accused the Student List of having "prepared terror" on election night. Pro-SNS Novosti mocks the new majority ("slept, ate"), while critical outlets call the day the "first day of liberating institutions". Event date: 17 November 2026.

## 2. Serbia

**[Military] Serbian Air Force MiG-29 crashes near Stara Pazova, killing Maj Goran Bulajić** — The 204th Air Brigade fighter came down at about 08:45 between Stara and Nova Pazova. Tango Six reports this was on short final approach to Batajnica runway 12L, in low cloud and fog. The 53-year-old pilot did not eject. Bulajić, known for rescuing more than 150 people in the 2014 Obrenovac floods, was close to retirement. The defence ministry formed an extraordinary-event commission and an Air Force technical commission, and the acting chief of the General Staff went to the site. Aviation editor Petar Vojinović said this is only the second MiG accident since 2006 and that all the MiG-29s have been overhauled and modernised. Event date: 17 November 2026.
