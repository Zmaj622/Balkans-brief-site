# Balkans Brief — 12 October 2026

**1. Kosovo**

**Legal opinions held up the Specialist Chambers amendment bill** — Three ministry/PM-office opinions, reported on 11 October, delayed the government's passage of the bill amending the Law on the Specialist Chambers to the Assembly. Justice says it needs specialised Assembly review because of its constitutional and international nature; Finance warns of extra costs for the judicial and prosecutorial councils; the PM's office addressed EU-law compatibility. Event date: 11 Oct 2026.

Sources: [Dukagjini](https://www.dukagjini.com/cka-shkruan-ne-opinionet-ligjore-te-ministrive-per-ndryshimet-e-ligjit-te-gjykates-speciale/) (No clear bias found) · [Lajmi.net](https://lajmi.net/cka-shkruan-ne-opinionet-ligjore-te-ministrive-per-ndryshimet-e-ligjit-te-gjykates-speciale/) (No clear bias found)

**Dismissed Special Prosecutor chief Isufaj weighs court appeal** — Blerim Isufaj, removed last week by the Kosovo Prosecutorial Council, is considering a court challenge after the 9-page reasoning was released: the Council says he mismanaged the office and did not use electronic random case allocation. The Council has not set a new competition; civil society calls the move political. Event date: 11 Oct 2026.

Sources: [Dukagjini](https://www.dukagjini.com/shkarkimi-i-blerim-isufajt-mund-te-shkoje-ne-gjykate-kpk-ja-ende-pa-plan-per-konkursin-e-ri/) (No clear bias found) · [Kosovo Online](https://www.kosovo-online.com/vesti/drustvo/bljerim-isufaj-razmatra-zalbu-na-smenu-tsk-jos-bez-odluke-o-novom-konkursu-11-10-2026) (Biased toward Serbia's ruling party (SNS) / Srpska Lista)

**2. Serbia**

**Dačić says he will run for president** — At the SPS rally in Belgrade Arena on 11 October, Ivica Dačić said that if presidential elections are called and he is a candidate he will "beat everyone", urging voters to back SPS on 25 October. He called the Students' List the "biggest obstacle" to stability. Event date: 11 Oct 2026.

Sources: [N1](https://n1info.rs/vesti/socijalisticka-partija-srbije-predizborni-skup-2026-ivica-dacic/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Danas](https://www.danas.rs/bbc-news-serbian/propascemo-malo-morgen-dacic-na-predizbornom-skupu-sps-u-beogradu/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS))

**Students demand names of 49 pardoned/amnestied people** — Students in Blockade said on 11 October that nearly two weeks after Vučić's pardon act the 26 pardoned names remain secret, asking whether Goran Vesić (Novi Sad canopy case), Nikola Selaković ("Generalštab") and Dijana Hrkalović (trading in influence) are included. The Higher Court and organized-crime prosecutor say they have received no pardon decisions. Event date: 11 Oct 2026.
