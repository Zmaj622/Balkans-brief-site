# Balkans Brief — 13 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**Gani Geci held for 48 hours over alleged threats to a journalist** — Kosovo Police detained Gani Geci on the evening of 12 October after journalist Lirim Mehmetaj said on TV that members of the Geci family threatened him when he refused to delete or change a post critical of Vetëvendosje MP Fatos Geci. Serious Crimes investigators questioned Geci, and the prosecutor ordered 48 hours' detention; Geci's son says he went to the police voluntarily. Event date: 12 October 2026.
