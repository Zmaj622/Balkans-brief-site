**[Students] [Political] Student List meets senior US diplomat Hanrahan and Russian ambassador Botsan-Kharchenko on the same evening** — US Assistant Secretary of State Brendan Hanrahan, on a multi-day visit to Belgrade, met representatives of the student movement and the Student List and congratulated them on their election win.
