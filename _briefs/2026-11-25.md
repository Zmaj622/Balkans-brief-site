# Balkans brief — 25 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Assembly elects committee members; SNS group stays out of all 20 committees** — The National Assembly voted through the decision on members and deputy members of 20 committees, with the Student List taking seats in all of them alongside minority MPs from the SDA of Sandžak, SPP and the Albanian Coalition. Aleksandar Vučić's "Serbia Wins" group, the Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians and the Sandžak Democratic Party stayed out; Vučić said he would not accept what he called the humiliation of parliamentary tradition. Student List MP Jelena Vasiljević Blagojević said committee chairs and deputy chairs are still not allocated and will be chosen at the committees' first sittings, asking how human rights, diaspora and security-service oversight committees could be entrusted to SNS. Speaker Aleksandar Čupić issued reprimands and twice cut Vučić's microphone; the session resumes today with elections to international delegations. Event date: 24 November 2026.
