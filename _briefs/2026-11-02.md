# Balkans brief — 2 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Repeat voting at 53 polling stations changes seat split: Zukorlić's list gets one mandate, Student List drops to 140** — Crta says the re-run of 1 November (turnout 44.9%) gave Usame Zukorlić's SPP–Movement for Unification list a seat on a margin of just 12 votes, taking one mandate from the Student List (now 140 of 250), with Vučić's list on 103; Crta also reports vote-buying allegations in favour of the Zukorlić list and irregularities. RIK published totals showing the Student List on 53.01% and "Aleksandar Vučić – Ujedinjena Srbija" on 38.98%; SNS-aligned outlets instead headline a 43.4% to 42.3% split in Vučić's favour, apparently the re-run polling stations only (not clear from the reports). Vučić said his list will take part in parliamentary work and will not obstruct the constitution of the Assembly. Event date: 1 November 2026.

**[OCG/Corruption] Kričak and Sekulović held up to 48 hours; deputy UKP chief and BIA/SBPOK head also questioned in "Senjak" case** — The prosecution ordered up to 48 hours' detention for police criminal-directorate chief Marko Kričak and crime-suppression head Boško Sekulović (arrested 1 November on suspicion of abuse of office, allegedly falsifying evidence and pressuring witnesses in the Senjak murder investigation); the UKP deputy chief was called for an informative interview, and the SBPOK head gave a statement. Vučić said it was "not wise" that the police leadership was not informed of the arrest and said it shows the state of institutions; Chief prosecutor Nenad Stefanović said the arrest has nothing to do with the elections, while Danas interlocutors expect further arrests in the security sector. Suspects are presumed innocent. Event date: 1 November 2026.
