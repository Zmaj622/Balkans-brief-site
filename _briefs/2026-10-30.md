# Balkans brief — 30 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Vučić denies Times report he weighed force after defeat, then attacks winners as "Taliban"** — At an SNS press conference Aleksandar Vučić rejected a Times (UK) report that he considered contesting the result by force. He said he told police chiefs before the vote he would concede "that second" if his list lost, and that no foreigner called him on election night. In the same address he called the Student List winners "Taliban" and "Jacobins", likened Student List figure Nemanja Karović to a "Gestapo commissar", and said SNS is starting a "fight for Serbia's freedom". Event date: 29 October 2026.

Sources: [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/vucic-tajms-drzavni-udar-izbori/) (No clear bias found) · [KoSSev, second report](https://kossev.info/vucic-posle-poraza-napao-studente-karovica-i-rts/) (No clear bias found)

**[Political] RIK orders repeat voting at 53 polling stations on 1 November; near-final result 53.04% to 38.97%** — The Republic Electoral Commission will rerun the vote at 53 stations (31 where results could not be established, 22 where voting was annulled), printing 24,438 ballots. On 8,338 of 8,392 stations counted, the Student List has 53.04% (2,349,689 votes) and Vučić's United Serbia 38.97% (1,726,375); only minority parties otherwise enter parliament. RIK also adopted the report on voting abroad: Student List 57,756 votes, United Serbia 19,763. Event date: 29 October 2026.

Sources: [RFE/RL Balkan Service](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/srbija-rik-ponavljanje-glasanja/33886458.html) (US-government funded) · [RTS](https://www.rts.rs/vesti/izbori-2026/6072060/rik-usvojio-izvestaj-o-glasanju-u-inostranstvu-najvise-glasova-studentskoj-listi.html) (Public broadcaster; widely accused of favouring SNS)

**[Political] Alleged destruction of documents in state bodies; Supreme Public Prosecutor orders police action** — A union representative at the PIO pension fund confirmed to RFE/RL that photos of bags of shredded documents are authentic ("something unusual is happening at night"); similar claims concern other ministries, BIA and VOA. Supreme Public Prosecutor Zagorka Dolovac ordered all chief prosecutors to ask police to prevent destruction of official files. The PIO fund says the shredding followed rules and protected citizens' personal data. Event date: 29 October 2026.

Sources: [RFE/RL Balkan Service](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/unistavanje-dokumentacije-drzavni-organi-izbori/33886712.html) (US-government funded) · [RFE/RL, prosecutor's order](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/srbija-vrhovna-tuziteljka-mere/33886729.html) (US-government funded)

**[Political] RTS apologises on air; students reject it and demand resignations; Student List rules out cooperation with Srpska Lista** — The new RTS news management apologised to viewers in the evening news a day after Karović accused the broadcaster of systematic deception; the programme ran late after a bomb threat, which police judged false. Students in blockade answered: "We accept only resignations." Separately, Student List's Jugoslav Gašić said there will be no political cooperation with Kosovo's Srpska Lista, which he equated with SNS policy. Event date: 29 October 2026.

## 2. Serbia

**[Political/Security] Prosecutors take over Israeli embassy attack case; terror threat at highest level** — The Organised Crime Prosecutor's Office took over the investigation into the 28 October attack in which a suspected minor with two knives attacked a Gendarmerie officer guarding the Israeli embassy and was shot dead; the interior minister called it terrorism and raised the terror threat level to red. ## 3. Bosnia and Herzegovina

**[Military] EUFOR helicopter crashes near Brčko: four dead, 11 injured** — An Italian-contingent EUFOR transport helicopter crashed in Gorice village near Brčko. EUFOR said two Italian service members and two Italian Military Red Cross personnel died; 11 injured are stable (nine in Brčko, two in Tuzla). EUFOR opened an investigation and urged against speculation. Condolences came from Meloni, Kaja Kallas, Komšić, Čović, KFOR and Kosovo's Konjufca; Italy's defence minister is due in BiH on 30 October. Event date: 29 October 2026.
