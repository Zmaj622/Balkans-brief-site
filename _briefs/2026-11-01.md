# Balkans brief — 1 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[OCG/Corruption] Head of Serbia's criminal police and head of the crime-suppression service arrested in "Senjak" case** — Marko Kričak (chief of the Criminal Police Directorate, UKP, former commander of the VIP-protection unit) and Boško Sekulović (head of the Crime Suppression Service) were arrested early on 1 November on orders of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Belgrade and held for 48 hours. They are suspected of abuse of office for allegedly not passing evidence to prosecutors in the May murder at restaurant "27" in Senjak, following a complaint by ex-Belgrade police chief Veselin Milić; Radar's Vuk Cvijić noted they were arrested by their own UKP subordinates. Pro-SNS Informer carries Dragan J. Vučićević's claim that the arrests are about "sensation"; critical outlets recall earlier complaints against Kričak (a student's allegation of threats and beatings after a 2025 protest). Event date: 1 November 2026.

Measures were renewed until 7 October; BIA director Vladimir Orlić reportedly asked on 29 October for them to stop. Unconfirmed by officials; single outlet chain.
