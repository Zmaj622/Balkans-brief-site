## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Repeat vote set for 1 November at 53 polling stations** — The Republic Electoral Commission (RIK) decided late on 28 October to rerun the election on Sunday 1 November (07:00–20:00) at 53 polling stations: 31 where results could not be established and 22 where voting was annulled, in places including Aleksinac, Zemun, Kruševac, Novi Pazar, Subotica and Ćuprija. 24,438 ballots will be printed.
