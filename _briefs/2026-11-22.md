# Balkans brief — 22 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Military] Ex-military union chief asks the General Staff who ordered 525 soldiers, including 160 "Sokolovi" special forces, onto the streets on election day** — Novica Antić, former president of the Military Trade Union of Serbia, filed a freedom-of-information request with the Serbian Armed Forces General Staff. He wants to know who decided to deploy 525 soldiers on 25 October, and on what legal basis. He says they included 160 armed members of the "Sokolovi" special operations battalion with special vehicles. He also asks whether 100 armed soldiers were stationed inside the National Assembly building, what role the Interior Ministry played, and whether the troops were ready to act against citizens. The request is one of the first public challenges to the army's role in the final days of SNS rule. The General Staff has not replied publicly. Event date: 21 November 2026 (reported; the request was sent on 19 November).

**[Political] Vučić: "pointless" for SNS to run in the presidential election; Student List says it has a candidate but won't name them yet** — Speaking to TV Informer from parliament, SNS group leader Aleksandar Vučić said it was pointless to take part in the coming presidential election under current conditions. If the SNS assembly decides to run, he said, that would be "against my will". He predicted the majority would find "10 small satellites" to lend the vote legitimacy, and Novosti's headlines quote him predicting no government before February. He also said SNS will decide whether to join Monday's consultations on committee posts once it sees the Student List's offer. Student List group leader Jovo Bakić told Nova the list "has a candidate, but we are not coming out with the name yet". He said SNS has no one to put forward and a new defeat would speed up its "disintegration". Bakić (head of the Student List parliamentary group; no further background is given in the report) said he had been preparing to be interior minister but it "does not mean it will be me". Event date: 21 November 2026.

**[Political] SNS complains to PACE, OSCE, the Venice Commission and ODIHR that the Student List took over the leadership of parliament** — In a letter signed by SNS international secretary Ana Brnabić, the party called the election of six deputy speakers, all from the Student List, on 19 November an "unprecedented, undemocratic takeover". It said this was the first time in 35 years of multiparty politics that the speaker and all the deputies came from one list. The letter adds that neither SNS, with almost 40% of the vote, nor the Hungarian, Bosniak and Albanian minority parties got a deputy speaker post. It warns the trend will continue when committee chairs are appointed next week. Nova instead profiles the new leadership, a mix of doctors, a professor, political scientists and an artist. It notes that minority representatives are among the deputies, but elected on the Student List ticket. Its interviewee calls the all-Student-List choice understandable but flags their lack of experience. Event date: 21 November 2026.

## 1. Kosovo

**[Military] KSF fires 120 mm Vuran mortar systems on live ranges at Bizë in Albania** — Batteries of the KSF Combat Support Regiment fired live rounds from 120 mm Alkar mortars mounted on Vuran armoured vehicles at the Bizë training area. Land Forces commander Brig Gen Mehmet Lladrovci observed the exercise with Albanian Land Forces representatives. Acting defence minister Ejup Maqedonci said some of the Vuran vehicles were donated by Turkey and the rest bought from the defence budget. He said the KSF is becoming "ever more powerful". Kosovo Online, which leans toward Belgrade, refers to the "so-called" KSF. Event date: 21 November 2026.
