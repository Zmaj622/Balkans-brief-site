# Balkans Morning News Briefing — 28 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Preliminary results: Student List 52.54%, SNS 39.29%; EU urges swift transition** — Preliminary results of the 25 October vote give the Student List 52.54% and Vučić's SNS 39.29%, according to Dukagjini. In a joint statement, EU foreign-policy chief Kaja Kallas and Enlargement Commissioner Marta Kos said the EU is ready to work with the next government and called for formal certification of results so parliament and government can form "without delay". European Council President António Costa also phoned Ilija Srdanović, who headed the Student List, to congratulate him. Event date: 27 October 2026.
