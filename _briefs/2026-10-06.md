# Balkans brief — 6 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**Assembly protest turns violent after Kurti–Hamza talks fail** — After PM Albin Kurti and PDK leader Bedri Hamza reached no deal on a second reading of the Specialist Chambers law amendments (PDK ties it to the presidential vote; Vetëvendosje wants the president elected first), police used tear gas and water cannon outside the Assembly as protesters threw flares and blocked MPs from leaving. Police say six officers were injured and 11 civilians sought medical help, with three detained; the presidential deadline expires on 6 October and protesters are due to regather. Pro-Serbian and pro-Serbian-government outlets call the protesters "KLA supporters"; Kosovo opposition outlets focus on alleged disproportionate police force and an MP demanding an inquiry. Event date: 5 October 2026.
