# Balkans brief — 26 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Informer owner Dragan J. Vučićević arrested for "causing panic" and obstruction of justice; SNS MPs walk out to rally at the police station** — Police detained the owner and editor of Informer, the loudest pro-SNS outlet, on the morning of 25 November on orders of the Second Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Belgrade. He is suspected of "causing panic and disorder" (a continuing offence) and obstruction of justice, is held for up to 48 hours, and will be questioned by the prosecutor today. Aleksandar Vučić announced the arrest in parliament, called it punishment for journalism, and Ana Brnabić, Miloš Vučević and other SNS MPs left the session to rally outside the Voždovac police station. Student List MPs applauded the announcement. SNS-aligned outlets describe Vučićević as a "political prisoner" and Vučić talks of a hunger strike if he is arrested himself. Critical outlets stress that Vučićević has repeatedly lost defamation cases and that the media regulator has started fining Informer TV since the election. Event date: 25 November 2026.

## 4. Montenegro
