# Balkans brief — 12 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[OCG/Corruption] Former economy minister Rade Basta arrested with seven others over Beogradske elektrane donations** — Police arrested Basta at Belgrade airport early on 11 November. He is suspected of abuse of office as director of the city heating company JKP Beogradske elektrane. The Interior Ministry says that between November 2020 and June 2022 he made ten unfounded decisions to donate 14.1 million dinars (about €119,000) to associations and sports clubs. A company finance director is suspected of aiding him, and six heads of sports clubs and associations are suspected of laundering the money. All eight were held for 48 hours for questioning by the Belgrade Higher Prosecutor's Office anti-corruption department. RFE/RL recalls that Basta, as minister in 2022–23, had argued for Russia sanctions. Event date: 11 November 2026.
