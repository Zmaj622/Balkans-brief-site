# Balkans brief — 21 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Čupić says he will call the presidential election by 27 November; the vote is expected on 27 December** — Speaker and acting president Aleksandar Čupić told FoNet the election must be called by 27 November and that he would do so "in the next few days", but not on 20 November. Vučić resigned on 27 September, and the law requires a vote within three months, with a campaign of 30 to 60 days. RT Balkan calculates that, because the election was not called on 20 November, Sunday 27 December is now the only legally possible date. Event date: 20 November 2026.

**[Political] Čupić's first foreign contacts as acting president: EU envoy pledges support, and he is invited to the 25 November EU–Western Balkans summit** — New EU ambassador Julian Vassallo hosted Čupić at the EU residence on his first full day in office. Vassallo said the parliament "has a steadfast supporter in the EU" in its democratic reforms. Čupić has been invited to represent Serbia at the EU–Western Balkans summit in Brussels on Wednesday, 25 November. ## 1. Kosovo

It lands just as the new Student List-led authorities take over in Belgrade. The border row with Montenegro is covered under Montenegro. Event date: 20 November 2026.

**[Political] JANAF restores crude deliveries to Serbia's NIS refinery in Pančevo after the pipeline accident** — JANAF said regular deliveries through the Adriatic pipeline resumed after a 24-hour repair of the Sisak–Slavonski Brod section damaged in an early-November accident. 568 cubic metres of spilled oil were recovered. While supply was cut, Serbia released 66,000 tonnes of crude from state reserves last week to keep the refinery running. Event date: 20 November 2026.
