# Balkans brief — 4 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Plane to Moscow turned back over Vulin; flight resumed.** — Air Serbia flight JU134 (Belgrade–Moscow) was recalled over Lithuania for extra security checks on one passenger. Nova.rs reports that former defence minister Aleksandar Vulin had been escorted aboard without passing baggage screening and that airport security chief Milan Kuč ordered the return; a prosecutor met the plane, and baggage was searched. Vulin says nothing illegal was found, calls it "harassment" and says he went to Moscow to negotiate gas supply. Dušan Bajatović (head of state gas company Srbijagas) says he went over gas and payment problems after the Alta banka collapse, and that all passengers re-boarded and the flight left again around 02:00 on 4 November. SNS-aligned outlets frame it as a political show of force by the new authorities; critical outlets stress the missed security check. Event date: 3 November 2026.

Sources: [N1](https://n1info.rs/vesti/n1-uzivo-na-aerodromu-nikola-tesla/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Vreme](https://vreme.com/vesti/aleksandar-vulin-er-srbija-let-za-moskvu-beogradski-aerodrom/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Srbija Danas](https://www.sd.rs/vesti/info/izivljavanje-aleksandar-vulin-o-vracanju-aviona-u-kome-je-putovao-za-moskvu-nista-nelegalno-nije-naden-kod-mene) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS))

**[OCG/Corruption] Top police officials Kričak and Sekulović held for up to 30 days.** — The Higher Court in Belgrade ordered up to 30 days' detention for Marko Kričak (head of the criminal police, UKP) and Boško Sekulović (head of the crime-suppression service), both suspended; they are suspected of abuse of office, and Kričak is also investigated over alleged ill-treatment of a man in May.
