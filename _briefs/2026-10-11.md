# Balkans Brief — 11 October 2026

**1. Kosovo**

**Kosovo says Radoičić's appearance at a Vučić rally proves Serbia's role in Banjska** — Kosovo's government told RFE/RL that Milan Radoičić, who has admitted organising the September 2023 Banjska attack in which a police officer was killed, was filmed next to Vučić's son Danilo outside an SNS rally in Kragujevac on 9 October. Pristina says it is "another clear proof" of state backing; Kosovo has issued five arrest warrants for him, and no indictment has been filed in Serbia. Event date: 10 October 2026 (government statement; rally 9 October).

Sources: [Evropa e Lirë (RFE/RL)](https://www.evropaelire.org/a/radoicic-vucic-qeveria-e-kosoves-/33874233.html) (US-government funded) · [Telegrafi](https://telegrafi.com/reagime-te-ashpra-nga-kosova/) (No clear party bias found)

**Srpska Lista leader Elek says he has joined Vučić's SNS and is no longer a Kosovo MP** — Zlatan Elek made the claim during a pre-election debate on Serbia's Nova TV, as reported by KoSSev. It was a verbal claim; no confirmation from the Kosovo Assembly was reported, so his formal status remains unverified. Event date: 10 October 2026.

Sources: [KoSSev via Indeksonline](https://indeksonline.net/kryetari-i-listes-serbe-kalon-ne-partine-e-vuciqit-nuk-jam-me-deputet-ne-kuvendin-e-kosoves/) (No clear bias found) · [Lajmi.net](https://lajmi.net/kreu-i-listes-serbe-pretendon-se-ka-kaluar-ne-sns-ne-e-vuciqit-sjam-me-deputet-ne-kuvendin-e-kosoves/) (No clear bias found)

**2. Serbia**

**Prosecutors appeal house arrest for three accused in the Senjak killing** — Belgrade's Higher Public Prosecutor's Office filed an appeal against the Higher Court's 2 October ruling that replaced detention with house arrest and electronic monitoring for three accused in the 12 May killing of Aleksandar Nešović at restaurant 27. A higher court will now decide. Event date: 10 October 2026.

Sources: Single source — [Nova](https://nova.rs/vesti/hronika/vjt-se-zalilo-sto-je-trojici-okrivljenih-za-ubistvo-na-senjaku-pritvor-zamenjen-nanogvicom/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS))

**Mladić flag incident at Student List rally in Niš** — At the Student List rally in Niš, a group of young men carried a flag with Ratko Mladić's image through the crowd; scuffles followed, the flag was removed and riot police intervened, with no detentions reported by N1/KoSSev. SNS-aligned outlets (Informer, Novosti) frame it as Student List officials attacking the men; KoSSev's account shows a crowd reaction and police leaving without arrests. Event date: 10 October 2026.

Sources: [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/skup-studentske-liste-nis-niska-tvrdjava/) (No clear bias found) · Novosti (Biased toward ruling party (SNS); state is largest shareholder) — headline only, link not retrieved

Sources: [Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/politika/umro-stevan-bakic-gradonacelnik/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Novosti](https://www.novosti.rs/vesti/politika/1658218/preminuo-gradonacelnik-subotice-stevan-bakic-59-godini) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS); state is largest shareholder)
