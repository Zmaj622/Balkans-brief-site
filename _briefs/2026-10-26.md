# Balkans brief — 26 October 2026

## Serbia election watch — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

**[Political] Vučić concedes as Student List wins** — Aleksandar Vučić, lead candidate of "Aleksandar Vučić – Ujedinjena Srbija", said late on 25 October "it is clear we lost" and congratulated the Student List, though he called the winners "contractors". He said Ujedinjena Srbija would have about 105 seats against 130–140 for the Students. Event date: 25 October 2026.

Sources: [Sd.rs / Srbija Danas](https://www.sd.rs/vesti/info/vucic-saopstio-rezultate-izbora-jasno-je-da-smo-mi-ove-izbore-izgubili-2026-10-25) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS)) · [Nova](https://nova.rs/vesti/svet/udarna-vest-u-bosanskim-medijima-vucic-priznao-poraz-i-rekao-da-ce-biti-promenjen-odnos-prema-rs/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/vucic-studentska-lista-najvise-mandata-izgubili-) (No clear bias found)

**[Political] CeSID/Ipsos projection: Student List 140 seats, Ujedinjena Srbija 103** — On a 96% sample, CeSID and Ipsos put the Student List at 52.9% and Ujedinjena Srbija at 38.8%. Dačić's list (2.2%), Autentična desnica (0.9%) and Evropska Srbija (0.3%) fell below the threshold. Minority lists entering: Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians 3 seats, Albanian Coalition 2, Rasim Ljajić's list 1, SDA Sandžaka 1. Official RIK results are not yet final. Event date: 25 October 2026.

Sources: [N1 live blog](https://n1info.rs/vesti/blog-srbija-vanredni-parlamentarni-izbori-25-oktobar-2026/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Kurir](https://www.kurir.rs/vesti/politika/10144533/projekcija-raspodele-mandata) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS))

**[Political] Dačić's SPS falls out of parliament** — Ivica Dačić said his Socialist Party (SPS) did not reach the threshold, "a lost battle", though over 100,000 people voted for it; he blamed his months-long illness. It is the first time since 1990 the SPS has no seats, per Klix. Event date: 25 October 2026.

Sources: [Sd.rs / Srbija Danas](https://www.sd.rs/vesti/info/dacic-sps-nije-ostvario-zeljeni-rezultat-na-izborima-nastavljamo-da-se-borimo-za-srb-2026-10-25) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS)) · [Nova](https://nova.rs/vesti/politika/dacic-na-poziciju-sps-je-uticala-moja-visemesecna-bolest) (Critical of ruling party (SNS))

**[Political] Irregularity claims on election day** — CRTA reported election day was marked by obstruction of polling boards.
