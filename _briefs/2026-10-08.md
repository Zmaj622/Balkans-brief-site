# Balkans brief — 8 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**EU and Germany object to amended Specialist Chambers law, hours after president elected** — The EU spokesperson said the amendments, passed late on 6 October just before Justina Shiroka-Pula was elected president, conflict with Kosovo's international obligations and called for the Chambers' independence and mandate to be respected; the German embassy also voiced "deep concern". PDK MP Përparim Gruda replied that Kosovo is not breaching obligations and the court "will not close". Shiroka-Pula was inaugurated on 7 October. Event date: 7 October 2026.

The Foreign Ministry proposed 90 stations abroad; critics say this is too few, and Renew Europe MEPs warned voters' rights are at risk. Event date: 7 October 2026.
