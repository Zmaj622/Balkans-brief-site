**[Political] RTS debate: Student List's Baucal says Kosovo's status is "a matter of negotiation"** — In the RTS programme "Takovska 10" on 13 October, Student List representative Aleksandar Baucal said Kosovo's status is "a matter of negotiation" and that citizens must sometimes "fight against their own state" to secure their rights; he also said the list will not impose sanctions on Russia but that foreign-policy alignment with the EU is itself "a matter of negotiation".
