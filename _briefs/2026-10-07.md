# Balkans brief — 7 October 2026

## 1. Kosovo

**Kosovo avoids snap elections: Justina Shiroka-Pula elected president as Specialist Chambers law is amended** — Hours before the constitutional deadline, the Assembly passed the amendments to the Law on the Specialist Chambers on second reading, then elected Justina Shiroka-Pula (nominated by PM Albin Kurti, accepted by PDK leader Bedri Hamza, who put up Eliza Hoxha as counter-candidate) with 81 votes; Hoxha got one. The two files had been tied together by PDK and AAK after the Hague court's convictions of four KLA leaders, and failure would have dissolved parliament and triggered the fourth election in under two years. Event date: 6 October 2026.

Sources: Single source — [Prishtina Insight (BIRN)](https://prishtinainsight.com/kosovo-elects-president-dodging-new-political-deadlock) (Western-donor funded). Headline-level corroboration from Telegrafi, Dukagjini, KoSSev, Kosovo Online and RFE/RL, not read in full.

**Two jailed after Pristina protest, one over alleged attack on an MP** — Pristina Basic Court ordered one month's detention each for Ekrem Seljimi and Fatljum Zogaj, arrested after the 5 October protest against the Hague court. Zogaj is suspected of assaulting an official in the incident where Vetëvendosje MP Fahruš Rexhepi was seized and moved over a police barrier. The prosecution has also authorised the police inspectorate (PIK) to examine police conduct during the protests (Kosovo Online headline). Event date: 6 October 2026.

Sources: Single source — [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/pritvor-dvojici-posle-protesta-jedan-osumnjicen-za-napad-na-poslanika/) (No clear bias found)

**Appeals court quashes Slađan Trajković war-crimes conviction** — The Pristina Court of Appeals annulled the conviction of former police officer Slađan Trajković and returned the case for retrial. Event date: 6 October 2026.

## 2. Serbia

**Vučić rally in Valjevo marked by counter-protest and rival accounts of violence** — At the SNS "United Serbia" rally, a police cordon separated rally-goers from protesting residents; student Vuk Vasiljević told N1 an inspector grabbed his arm and tried to take his phone. Pro-government outlets and SNS leader Miloš Vučević say supporters of the "Student List" attacked two women (Nina Kuburović, Tanja Zdravković), pulling hair and pouring water, and demand arrests; critical outlets focus on police conduct towards protesters. Election is 25 October. Event date: 6 October 2026.

Sources: [N1](https://n1info.rs/english/news/vucic-in-valjevo-as-local-residents-protest-student-says-police-tried-to-take-his-phone/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)) · [Srbija Danas](https://www.sd.rs/vesti/info/dno-politike-bezumlja-i-agresije-milos-vucevic-ostro-osudio-skandalozni-napad-na-zene-u-valjevu-hitno-pohapsiti-i-sankcionisati-nasilnike-video-2026-10-06) (Biased toward ruling party (SNS))
