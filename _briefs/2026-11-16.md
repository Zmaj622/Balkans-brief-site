## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Military] Student List sets out its defence plan: keep modernising, aim for 3% of GDP, re-examine procurement and make conscription depend on a staffing review** — RTS broadcast in full an interview with Luka Ponorac, a Student List representative and member of its defence programme group. Ponorac is a Military Academy graduate and former technical-service officer. He said the new authorities will keep modernising the army, but procurement must go through proper channels: requirements should come from the units and design work from the Military Technical Institute, "not some trading companies". He said cutting the defence budget would be "politically irresponsible". The current 2–2.5% of GDP should rise towards 3%, with the 5% that neighbouring NATO members have committed to as a reference. He said the plan to reintroduce compulsory service looked "more populist than the product of analysis". It should depend on a review of whether there are enough officers to train conscripts, he said, since about 60 of the 158 officers in his 2015 class have left. The new majority would not say who will run the ministry. The interview was recorded on 6 November for the MoD–RTS series "Dozvolite…" but aired separately because the ministry broke off cooperation with RTS. Event date: 16 November 2026 (full broadcast; interview recorded 6 November).
