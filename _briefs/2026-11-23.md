# Balkans brief — 23 November 2026

## Serbia transition watch — after the 25 October 2026 election

**[Political] Čupić: presidential election to be called by 26 November and held on 27 December; the Student List's candidate is decided but not yet named** — Aleksandar Čupić is Speaker and acting president. In his first RTS interview he said the vote would be called within days, and on N1's "Utisak nedelje" he said it will be held on 27 December, "the only possible date". He blamed his predecessor Ana Brnabić for not calling it earlier. Asked whether the Student List's plenums had settled on a candidate, he said "it is known who it is" but gave no name. He said the Law on Ministries will be amended to cut the number of ministries, and that "a good government is more important than a quick one". He said the handover with Brnabić, the former Assembly secretary-general and the presidency has still not taken place. He explained his absence from Wednesday's EU–Western Balkans summit in Brussels: it would be "irresponsible" while the Assembly has not finished even five technical agenda items. The constitutive session resumes on Tuesday with election of committee members. Event date: 22 November 2026.

## 1. Kosovo

**[Political] Serbian Orthodox Church accuses Vetëvendosje MP Arber Rexhaj of hate speech over the cultural-heritage law** — The Raška-Prizren Eparchy condemned remarks by Vetëvendosje MP Arber Rexhaj (Redžaj) during the overnight debate on 19–20 November. He claimed Orthodox churches had served as "dungeons and bunkers for eliminating Albanians", and that the Ahtisaari plan had declared "all Albanian Orthodox heritage" Serbian. The eparchy says the remarks bear the hallmarks of hate speech and make monks, clergy and believers fear for their safety. It noted that neither the presiding speaker nor the culture minister reacted, and asked international representatives to raise the matter with the Assembly leadership. Srpska Lista's Igor Simić told KoSSev that the translation in the chamber was poor and that he had answered Rexhaj. He also criticised the student movement, Nenad Rašić and the new authorities in Belgrade. Event date: 22 November 2026 (statement; the remarks were made on 19–20 November).

**[Political] Kurti meets Albanian leaders in Tetovo while he and Rama keep separate agendas in Bitola** — Kurti met Tetovo mayor Bilall Kasami and Izet Mexhiti to discuss political developments in North Macedonia, the political representation of Albanians there and infrastructure links. ## 2. Serbia

**[Political] Belgrade city assembly votes today on dismissing Mayor Šapić; NPS says only snap elections can end the crisis** — The motion was brought by Kreni-Promeni and other groups after Kreni-Promeni councillor Tatjana Mićić was seriously injured. ## 3. Bosnia and Herzegovina

**[Political] Igor Dodik named RS prime minister-designate; his father announces an SNSD blockade of the state Council of Ministers** — RS President Savo Minić named SNSD organisational secretary Igor Dodik, Milorad Dodik's son, as prime minister-designate. Minić called him the youngest in RS history, and expects the government to be formed by mid-December. Igor Dodik promised transparency and accountability, "special" cooperation with "brotherly Serbia" and its new government. He said RS is for the EU "only as much as we are respected by it". Coalition partners Stevandić's party and DNS–NPS confirmed they will join. N1 BiH reports Milorad Dodik saying that, from now on, no SNSD member of the Council of Ministers will take any decision until the Constitutional Court is changed and its recent rulings withdrawn. N1 BiH does not say when he said it. Opposition figures speak of a "dynasty" and a "monarchy", while SNSD-aligned SRNA and ATV carry a stream of endorsements. Event date: 22 November 2026.

## 4. Montenegro

**[Political] Croatian PM's adviser Granić: "no negotiations" on the land border with Montenegro** — Mate Granić is a former Croatian foreign minister and Plenković's special foreign-policy adviser. He told HRT that Croatia will be "hard as stone" on the land border, which he says the Badinter Commission fixed in 1991. Croatia strongly supports Montenegro's EU membership, he said, but the border is among Plenković's eight open issues that Podgorica must resolve before Croatia gives its consent. Montenegrin opposition and pro-Serbian outlets (IN4S) call Zagreb's stance arrogance and an ultimatum. Event date: 22 November 2026.

Sources: Single source — [Dan](https://www.dan.co.me/vijesti/granic-hrvatska-ce-biti-tvrda-kao-kamen-o-kopnenoj-granici-s-crnom-gorom-nema-pregovora/) (Right-leaning, pro-Serbian) (HRT and Aktuelno carry it, per their headlines)

**[Political] Montenegro's EU accession treaty to include a ten-year "probation" with suspendable voting rights, Politico reports** — Citing Politico, Dan reports that EU ambassadors broadly backed a compromise proposed by the Irish Council presidency. It would put a safeguard in chapter 35 of Montenegro's accession treaty: a ten-year monitoring period, extendable by five years. During that time Montenegro's vote could be suspended by qualified majority if it seriously breaches the rule of law or obstructs common decisions. The Commission had proposed 15 years, and Italy and the "Friends of the Western Balkans" pushed for less. The reports do not say when the ambassadors agreed. Event date: reported 22 November 2026.

Sources: Single source — [Dan](https://www.dan.co.me/vijesti/politika/tsrnoj-gori-spriemaju-najduzhi-pripravnichki-stazh-u-ieu/) (Right-leaning, pro-Serbian) (RT Balkan and Kosovo Online carry it, per their headlines)
