# Balkans Hybrid Activity Report — week ending 2026-10-31

## Key points
- Montenegrin police acted against alleged organising of Serbian-election voters on its territory: Vijesti says three people were detained in Berane (25 Oct) and police searched eight flats in Budva linked to Miloš Janjušević (26 Oct), reportedly an SNS voter coordinator. A duty prosecutor was to assess whether a crime occurred; no charges are logged.
- Serbia's 25 October election drew diaspora-voter allegations in two countries: KRIK says an SNS-linked association in Vienna irregularly registered Serbian voters, and Vijesti says a Serbian Embassy vehicle was involved in the Montenegro case. Both are claims, not established facts.
- Pattern: the same actor (SNS-linked networks) and method (political interference through diaspora voters) appear in Austria and Montenegro. This follows earlier logged reporting (BIRN, 18 and 21 Oct) that Serbia's foreign ministry funded SNS-supporting diaspora groups.
- No entries were logged for Russia, China or any other state this week, and nothing was logged on 28–31 October.

## Serbia

**Serbian-election voter coordination in Montenegro (Berane/Budva)** — Countries: Montenegro. Methods: political interference. Status: searched (police searches of Budva flats, 26 Oct).
- What is reported: Vijesti, citing unofficial sources, says Montenegrin police in Berane detained Goran Janjušević, Radovan Guzina and a Serbian citizen on suspicion of interfering in the voting of Serbian citizens, and found lists with dozens of names. It says Miloš Janjušević, described as an unofficial coordinator of pro-SNS voters, left in a Serbian Embassy vehicle. Police then searched eight Budva flats linked to him on suspicion of voting-rights violations; Vijesti's sources say the flats were meant to house people to influence a coming Montenegrin election.
- Timeline:
  - 2026-10-25 — Vijesti says (citing unnamed sources) police in Berane detained three people and found voter lists; says Miloš Janjušević left in a Serbian Embassy vehicle. A duty prosecutor was to assess whether a crime occurred.
  - 2026-10-26 — Police search eight Budva flats linked to Miloš Janjušević on suspicion of voting-rights violations; Vijesti's sources say the flats were meant to house people to influence a coming Montenegrin election.
- People: Goran Janjušević (suspected, detained); Radovan Guzina (suspected, detained); Miloš Janjušević (suspected, wanted/searched). Organisations: SNS (political party). State bodies acting: Police Directorate Montenegro (police); Berane Police (police); Embassy of Serbia in Montenegro (embassy, named in the claim).
- Targets: Serbian parliamentary election voting in Montenegro; Montenegrin elections.
- Related crime cases: ME-JANJUSEVIC-ELECTION — Janjušević searches over election influence.

**Diaspora voter registration in Austria** — Countries: Serbia (diaspora voters in Austria). Methods: political interference. Status: claimed.
- What is reported: KRIK, citing recordings, says the Serbian Association in Austria, close to the SNS, collected documents and registered Serbian citizens to vote through unofficial channels while urging support for the ruling party, a role legally belonging to diplomatic missions. Reported via Dukagjini on 24 October, ahead of the 25 October election.
- Timeline:
  - 2026-10-24 — KRIK says the association irregularly registered diaspora voters (reported via Dukagjini; logged 25 Oct).
- People: none named. Organisations: Association of Serbs in Austria (association); SNS (political party); KRIK (investigative outlet, source of the claim). State bodies acting: none named.
- Targets: Serbian diaspora voters in Austria.
- Related crime cases: none.
- Sources: Single source — [Dukagjini](https://www.dukagjini.com/skandal-ne-vjene-krik-zbulon-rrjetin-e-lidhur-me-vuciqin-qe-dyshohet-se-manipulon-votuesit-e-diaspores-serbe/) (No clear bias found).
