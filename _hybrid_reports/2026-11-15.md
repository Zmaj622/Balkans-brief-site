# Balkans Hybrid Activity Report — week ending 2026-11-14

## Key points
- No official measures (expulsions, arrests, sanctions) were logged this week. The most serious development is in Serbia: weekly Radar reports that three senior BIA officials were removed over BIA surveillance of journalists, and that one of them refused an order to rewrite documentation so leaked surveillance documents could be called forgeries. BIA denies the dismissals and any falsification of documents, and calls the reporting part of "hybrid attacks" on Serbia (11 Nov).
- Two RFE/RL investigations describe sanctions evasion routed through Serbia. About €27.5 million of equipment made by Grindex of Kikinda reportedly reached sanctioned Russian defence firms via intermediaries in 2022–2025 (10 Nov). Republika Srpska reportedly limited a €6 million tractor subsidy to tractors from sanctioned Belarusian state company Minsk Tractor Works after a Dodik–Lukashenko deal, with about 250 tractors imported via a Serbian firm founded by Belarus's honorary consul in Novi Sad (12 Nov).
- In Bosnia and Herzegovina, NATO HQ Sarajevo officials Vladimir Vučinić and Brigadier General James Fowler say BiH has been targeted by cyber attacks and Russia-linked disinformation, and that further hybrid attacks are likely (13 Nov). This is their claim.
- Pattern: Serbia appears as a transit hub or base in three of the five entries (Grindex, the Belarusian tractor imports, and Montenegrin claims of Serbian hybrid attacks). This follows earlier logged claims that Serbia served as a hub for Russian sabotage logistics (WSJ, 21 Oct). Nothing was logged for China.

## Russia

**Grindex equipment reaching sanctioned Russian military industry** — Countries: Serbia. Methods: sanctions evasion. Status: reported (10 Nov).
- What is reported: RFE/RL reports, citing Ukrainian military intelligence (HUR) data and its own analysis of financial records, that equipment worth about €27.5 million made by Grindex of Kikinda was imported into Russia between March 2022 and March 2025. It says the equipment went through intermediary companies, some under US and EU sanctions, and was then supplied to sanctioned Russian defence firms. Grindex and the Russian buyers did not respond.

## Serbia

**Reported BIA purge over surveillance of Radar journalists** — Countries: Serbia. Methods: espionage, intimidation. Status: reported (11 Nov); dismissals denied by BIA.
- What is reported: Weekly Radar reports that BIA deputy director Tomislav Radovanović, First Directorate head Miloš Simić and chief of staff Olivera Munjić were removed, and links this to revelations of BIA surveillance of journalists. Radar says Simić refused an order to rewrite documentation on the surveillance of Radar journalists so that leaked documents could be called forgeries. BIA denies the dismissals and any destruction or falsification of documents, and calls Radar's reporting part of "hybrid attacks" on Serbia's security system. The dismissals and the order to falsify documents rest on Radar's reporting; the surveillance of journalists is the context of that reporting.

- People: Tomislav Radovanović (BIA deputy director); Miloš Simić (head of BIA First Directorate); Olivera Munjić (BIA chief of staff). Organisations: Radar (weekly). State bodies acting: BIA (Security Information Agency).
- Targets: Radar journalists; critics of the former government.

## Belarus

**Republika Srpska subsidy limited to sanctioned Belarusian tractors** — Countries: Bosnia and Herzegovina (Republika Srpska), with imports via Serbia. Methods: sanctions evasion, economic leverage. Status: reported (12 Nov).
- What is reported: An RFE/RL investigation found that Republika Srpska limited a €6 million tractor subsidy scheme to tractors from Minsk Tractor Works, a Belarusian state company sanctioned by Ukraine and several Western states, following a deal between Milorad Dodik and Aleksandr Lukashenko. Organisations: Minsk Tractor Works (Belarusian state company); Agro-Simeks (company); Agropanonka (company); SNSD (political party). State bodies acting: Government of Republika Srpska; Ministry of Agriculture of Republika Srpska.
- Targets: Western sanctions on Belarus.
- Related crime cases: none.
