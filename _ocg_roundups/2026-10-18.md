# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-10-17

## Key points
- **Serbia's organised-crime prosecutors opened an investigation against 12 suspected members of the "Vračarci" group** over the kidnap-killings of Milan Stoković and Živko Bakić (one detainee reportedly confessed; suspects are held in Serbia and North Macedonia), while a single pro-government outlet reports moves to confiscate assets of the Janković brothers, described as Škaljari members.
- **Kosovo–Serbia: the Banjska case resurfaced.** Milan Radoičić, who admitted organising the 2023 Banjska attack and is wanted by Kosovo, was filmed at an SNS rally in Kragujevac; Kosovo says this shows Serbian state backing, while Belgrade prosecutors say their investigation remains open.
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina's FBiH corruption prosecutions advanced on three fronts:** indictments were confirmed against ex-Autoceste FBiH director Elmedin Voloder and three others (damage over KM 2.53 million) and against former Sarajevo Airport managers, FBiH premier Nermin Nikšić was questioned as a suspect in the Spengavanje case, and the FBiH Supreme Court barred minister Vojin Mijatović from office as a precaution. SIPA and Republika Srpska police also made organised-crime and migrant-smuggling arrests.
- **Montenegro: indictments and official admissions of leaks.** Indictments were filed over the Pejović and Roganović murders (the latter linked by Kurir to Krsta Vujić's group) and ten people were indicted over cocaine hidden in soy flour. The police director said "moles" leaked information to organised crime groups during the Šabunjin manhunt, and that the Herceg Novi hideout of wanted Drecun, Medenica and Tanaskovski held an arsenal. A court also ruled Svetozar Marović's sentence time-barred.
- **Albania: a run of violent incidents with suspected criminal links** — a car explosion at Gjirokastër, a TNT blast at lawyer Idajet Beqiri's car in Tirana, and three arrests in operation "Detajet" over a foiled contract killing (remanded 12 October) — alongside the 7.4-year sentence of ex-deputy border chief Stiljan Pasho for facilitating cannabis smuggling.
- **Officials under scrutiny in Serbia and elsewhere:** Belgrade mayor Aleksandar Šapić faces a prosecutors' pre-investigation over an alleged assault on a councillor; a former SNS activist's vote-buying and paid-groups claims target the Novosadska toplana director; Croatia's USKOK questioned Oroslavje mayor Viktor Šimunić and opened a six-person cigarette-smuggling investigation.
