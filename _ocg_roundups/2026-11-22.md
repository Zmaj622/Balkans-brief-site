# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-11-21

## Key points
- **Serbia — investigations reach senior officials after the election:** organised-crime prosecutors (TOK) confirmed that police questioned caretaker Justice Minister Nenad Vujić and seized his phone and that of prisons chief Mile Jandrić, over a complaint alleging they ran a criminal group that bribed inmates to vote SNS. The Anti-Corruption Agency filed criminal complaints against Belgrade mayor Aleksandar Šapić (assets) and former energy minister Dubravka Đedović Handanović (undeclared Croatian company). Prosecutors are also seeking at least three years for Šapić over the assault on councillor Tatjana Mićić. Six Leskovac city officials were arrested over procurement. Police refused to carry out a prosecutor's order to arrest Belgrade Waterfront's representative. All are allegations or suspicions only.
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina — top Federation officials indicted:** POSKOK indicted FBiH prime minister Nermin Nikšić, interior minister Ramo Isak and five others (Spengavanje) for criminal association, abuse of office and trading in influence over the appointment of police director Vahidin Munjić. The indictment still needs court confirmation. SIPA arrested its own inspector Jelena Mičić on suspicion of leaking investigation data; her brother is described as linked to criminal groups. About 227,000 litres of army fuel (€419,000) are missing.
- **Albania — SPAK's biggest cases advance:** SPAK asked the court to try former deputy PM Belinda Balluku, adding corruption and money laundering to the charges and alleging €81.6m of damage on the Llogara tunnel alone. It also sought confiscation of €9.1m from Elvis Doçi's Durrës cocaine organisation, and the Special Court detained Kevin Margjoni after his extradition from Spain over the 485 kg Fier cocaine lab. New probes target the husband of ex-speaker Elisa Spiropali and a suspected 3.6bn-lek hole at Albanian Post.
- **Kosovo and the Banjska suspect:** a former LDK culture minister, Astrit Haraqija, was among six arrested in operation 'Tišina' against a Pristina cocaine network (his lawyer denies involvement). Belgrade's Higher Court issued a warrant for Banjska suspect Milan Radoičić after he failed to report to police, and BiH banned him and the US-sanctioned Veselinović brothers as security threats.
- **Montenegro — clan violence and justice-system links:** alleged Škaljari clan member Darko Vicković was shot dead in Nikšić. At the trial of ex-chief special prosecutor Milivoje Katnić, SKY messages naming Belivuk, Šarić and Kavač leader Radoje Zvicer were read out. The 'General' 2.5-tonne cocaine trial (Zvicer, ex-Budva mayor Milo Božović) must restart. Goran Gogić pleaded guilty in New York over about 20 tonnes of cocaine.
- **North Macedonia — criminals as proxies for a state actor:** six Macedonian citizens described as established in criminal structures allegedly ran logistics, on an Iranian national's instructions, for a planned attack in Greece. Four were arrested in North Macedonia and one in Greece.
