# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-10-31

## Key points
- **Serbia's organised-crime prosecutors turned inward:** TOK demanded the full file on 49 pardons granted by Aleksandar Vučić, to check whether any pardoned person was accused in organised-crime proceedings, and opened cases against ambassadors over alleged election-law violations abroad.
- **Banjska attack back in the courts:** Kosovo's Special Prosecution indicted Stefan Radulović for terrorism (26 Oct), and a commission of Serbia's Supreme Public Prosecutor's Office is reviewing why suspect Milan Radoičić was not detained after questioning.
- **Albania, two prosecution milestones:** SPAK sent 17 members of the Burba–Shuli cocaine and marijuana group to trial, and concluded its investigation into former deputy PM Belinda Balluku (charges include corruption and money laundering) without renewing its arrest request.
- **Kosovo: sitting mayor of Prishtina, Përparim Rama, questioned as a suspect** for abuse of official position in the "Capital Architect" case; separately a German national was detained over EUR 1 million seized at Merdare.
- **Montenegro:** the High Court confirmed the indictment of Duško Knežević for forming a criminal organisation in the E-commerce / Atlas Bank case (about 63 million euros blocked).
- **Pattern, election-related prosecutions:** Serbian ambassadors (TOK) and a Budva voter-coordination case in Montenegro both concern alleged voting-rights violations.

### Serbia
**Serbian organised-crime prosecutor review of Vučić pardons** (RS-VUCIC-PARDONS-49) — Stage at end of week: investigation. OCG: suspected (reported). Corruption: no.
- What is reported: TOK asked the Ministry of Justice to hand over urgently the complete documentation on the pardoning of 49 people announced by then-president Aleksandar Vučić, including correspondence with the president's cabinet. ### Kosovo
**Banjska attack and Milan Radoičić** (XK-BANJSKA-ATTACK) — Stage at end of week: indictment. OCG: suspected (signature). Corruption: no.
- What is reported: Kosovo's Special Prosecution filed an indictment with the Basic Court in Pristina against Stefan Radulović of North Mitrovica for terrorism and serious crimes against the constitutional order over the 24 September 2023 Banjska attack; he was arrested at Merdare in April and is in detention. The prosecution says he acted as a member of an organised, heavily armed group; an earlier indictment named 45 people. In Serbia, a Supreme Public Prosecutor's Office commission is examining why suspect Milan Radoičić was not detained after questioning in Belgrade; the prosecutor reportedly cited only flight risk.
- Timeline: 2026-10-26 — Radulović indicted in Pristina. 2026-10-30 — Serbian Supreme Prosecutor's commission reviews the decision not to seek detention for Radoičić.
- People: Stefan Radulović (suspect, indicted); Afrim Bunjaku (police sergeant, killed); Milan Radoičić (suspected); Nikola Pantelić (prosecutor).
- Organisations: none named this week. Institutions acting: Special Prosecution of Kosovo; Basic Court Pristina; Kosovo Police; Supreme Public Prosecutor's Office of Serbia; Higher Public Prosecutor's Office Belgrade.
- Amounts: none.
