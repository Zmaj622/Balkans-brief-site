# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-10-10

## Key points
- **Croatia's Gospić illegal-waste case widened sharply:** on 9 October USKOK-ordered police arrested five people in four towns on suspicion of serious environmental crimes within a criminal association, including jeweller Paško Dodić and Ce-za-r board member Željko Teufel (both deny guilt). The week also brought a parliamentary row over what the SOA intelligence agency knew in 2022-23.
- **Regional drug-network blow:** Europol reported nearly 50 arrests (34 in Slovenia) in a Western Balkans-linked drug network tied to shootings, murders and a kidnapping; suspects include nationals of Bosnia and Herzegovina and Serbia. Montenegro also received Aleksandar Paunović from Serbia by extradition over the 2020 Stanjevića Rupa double murder.
- **Montenegro's prosecutors target criminal assets and security-sector figures:** SDT is seeking permanent confiscation of property of convicted Kavač clan boss Radoje Zvicer and of convicted Milan Vujotić, the Rokšped cigarette-smuggling accused was granted €1.22m bail (SDT appealed), police arrested the ANB union head in a probe of security-sector unions, and five Russians linked to Rybar were expelled.
- **Albania's SPAK is running several politically sensitive probes:** the Llogara tunnel tender case against former deputy PM Belinda Balluku (defendants re-questioned after new e-mails; threat allegations by a cooperating official's father), the KAYO arms companies search of Ron Yeffet, a civil-registry identity-change probe tied to AKSHI computers, and a report on Signal contacts between ex-SHISH head Vlora Hyseni and fugitive-turned-detainee Ergys Agasi (no charge reported).
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina:** seven indicted over the Tuzla pensioners' home fire that killed 15; a police officer is among four detained over a 15-tonne copper-wire theft; the Court of BiH confirmed an indictment against priest Petar Barbarić for glorifying Slobodan Praljak and acquitted Milan Mijić of 1992 crimes.
- **Pattern of attacks and threats against journalists and activists in Serbia:** N1's Mladen Savatović was threatened again, a Zoomer reporter was hit in Niš, and a campaigning student was cut with a scythe in Grocka; in the Senjak murder case, the indicted deputy police director Veselin Milić remains in post per the interior minister.
