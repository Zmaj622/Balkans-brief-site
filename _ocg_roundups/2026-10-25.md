# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-10-24

## Key points
- **Albania:** SPAK communicated final charges on five offences to former deputy PM Belinda Balluku (tender rigging, passive corruption, false asset declarations, money laundering) on 20 October; charges are being communicated to others in the same case. SPAK also seized about EUR 800,000 in assets of two members of the Çopja drug group.
- **Serbia:** KRIK reports that 15 narcotics officers were sidelined after the record five-ton marijuana seizure at Konjuh, on a farm owned by an SNS councillor. The officers' letter accuses UKP chief Marko Kričak of destroying the unit (the claims are KRIK's reporting of the letter). Separately, a Serbian national, Milan Tanović, was sentenced in Texas to 204 months for cocaine smuggling with Serbian and Albanian traffickers.
- **Courts and officials:** Kosovo's Court of Appeals confirmed the indictment of Deputy PM Fikrim Damka; Montenegro's Higher Court confirmed the indictment of ex-defence minister Predrag Bošković; the Court of BiH acquitted ex-OSA director Osman Mehmedagić at first instance; and FBiH premier Nermin Nikšić and Ramo Isak were questioned by POSKOK. Sitting or former senior officials were in the dock in four countries.
- **Bosnia:** Sarajevo police arrested Haris Karadža, reported as close to indicted Adnan Maglić and linked to the Kavač clan, over a December 2025 attempted murder. Separately, the escort of a Zenica prison employee was arrested with drugs and a pistol.
- **Kosovo:** Ten people were detained in a joint operation against smuggling from Serbia in the north, and two extraditions were reported (a drug suspect to Sweden, a fraud suspect back to Kosovo).

## PART A — ORGANISED CRIME

### Serbia
**Konjuh five-ton marijuana seizure and narcotics unit purge** (RS-KONJUH-MARIJUANA) — Stage at end of week: allegation. OCG: suspected (signature). Corruption: yes.
- What is reported: KRIK reports that 15 officers of the Criminal Police Directorate's narcotics division were removed after the five-ton marijuana seizure at Konjuh near Kruševac, on a farm owned by a local SNS councillor. The officers wrote to Prime Minister Đuro Macut alleging that UKP chief Marko Kričak destroyed the unit and that they face retaliation. The claims are KRIK's reporting of the officers' letter.
- Timeline: 2026-10-19 — KRIK reports the officers' letter and the sidelining of the 15 officers.
- People: Marko Kričak (police official, accused of retaliation); Đuro Macut (prime minister); Radomir Popović (former narcotics unit head, sidelined); Nikola Đoković (deputy unit head, sidelined).
- Organisations: SNS (political party). Institutions acting: UKP (Criminal Police Directorate); MUP Serbia (ministry of interior).
- Amounts: 5 tons of marijuana.
- Sources: [KRIK](https://www.krik.rs/pismo-policajaca-iz-operacije-konjuh-od-zaplene-5-tona-marihuane-pocinje-unistenje-sluzbe-za-borbu-protiv-droga/) (No clear bias found); [N1](https://n1info.rs/english/news/krik-15-narcotics-officers-sidelined-after-historic-drug-bust/) (Critical of ruling party (SNS)).

**Milan Tanović cocaine smuggling** (RS-TANOVIC-COCAINE) — Stage at end of week: sentence. OCG: yes (reported). Corruption: no.
- What is reported: KoSSev and Danas report that a US federal court in Texas sentenced Milan Tanović (59) on 15 October to 204 months (17 years) after he pleaded guilty to conspiracy to possess cocaine for distribution. US prosecutors say he worked with traffickers from Serbia and Albania moving cocaine to Europe by container ship; he was linked via encrypted chat analysis. Reported 23 October. (Serbian and Albanian traffickers also appear in Albania's section below.)
- Timeline: 2026-10-15 — sentenced in Texas; reported by Serbian media on 23 October.
- People: Milan Tanović (convicted).
- Organisations: none named. Institutions acting: US federal court, Eastern District of Texas.
- Amounts: none reported (sentence 204 months).
- Sources: Single source — [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/drzavljanin-srbije-osudjen-u-sad-na-17-godina-zatvora-sa-krijumcarima-iz-srbije-i-albanije-prebacivao-tone-kokaina-u-evropu/) (No clear bias found).

### Kosovo
**Kosovo north goods smuggling investigation** (XK-NORTH-SMUGGLING) — Stage at end of week: detention. OCG: suspected (signature). Corruption: no.
- What is reported: Kosovo Police deputy regional director Veton Elshani told RFE/RL that ten people were arrested in a joint action against smuggling of goods from Serbia via illegal routes in the north; all were released after questioning and will be prosecuted in regular procedure. The Mitrovica Basic Prosecution said a six-month investigation led to the seizure of over 22 vehicles and goods worth over EUR 200,000. Serbia-origin goods are involved; no Serbian group is named.
- Timeline: 2026-10-22 — ten detained in joint operation in Zubin Potok, Zvečan, Leposavić and North Mitrovica; later released.
- People: none named (Veton Elshani, police deputy regional director, quoted).
- Organisations: none. Institutions acting: Kosovo Police; Basic Prosecution Mitrovica (prosecutor's office); Kosovo Intelligence Agency; Kosovo Customs.
- Amounts: over EUR 200,000 in goods; over 22 vehicles.
- Sources: Single source — [RFE/RL Balkan Service](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/kosovo-hapsenje-krijumcarenje-/33881618.html) (US-government funded).
