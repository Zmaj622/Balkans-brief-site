# Balkans Crime & Corruption Round-up — week ending 2026-11-07

## Key points
- **Serbia's police leadership under arrest:** criminal police chief Marko Kričak and crime-suppression head Boško Sekulović were arrested on 1 Nov on suspicion of abuse of office in the Senjak restaurant murder case; on 3 Nov the Higher Court ordered up to 30 days' custody. On 6 Nov the court also ordered detention for SAJ commander Igor Žmirić and eight others over alleged torture of detained students in the Government garage (August 2025).
- **Alta banka collapse:** after the central bank revoked the licence (31 Oct), owner Davor Macura was detained by TOK on suspicion of taking about 45 billion dinars (about EUR 383m) from the bank's treasury; a prosecution document cited by KRIK and Nova puts the figure near EUR 390m. North Macedonia and Montenegro acted against Alta units. Prosecutors seek up to 30 days' detention.
- **Election-related cases around the BIA and SNS:** Radar alleges BIA wiretapped TOK prosecutors; TOK opened two cases on complaints against BIA chief Vladimir Orlić and caretaker Justice Minister Nenad Vujić over voting-rights offences; Zabela prison warden Rodoljub Rončević was arrested over alleged vote buying. All are allegations or suspicions only.
- **Alleged links between criminal groups and officials (unproven):** the Student List asked prosecutors to investigate Sky ECC-based allegations linking Aleksandar Vučić to the Šarić and Belivuk groups; courts published the names of those pardoned by Vučić, including Dijana Hrkalović.
- **Albania:** SPAK sought a combined 292 years for 14 defendants in the "Golden Bullet" case (35 years for Henrik Hoxha); Balkanweb published an unverified photo of euros allegedly found in ex-deputy PM Belinda Balluku's villa; Erion Veliaj claims PM Rama relayed threats to him in prison.
- **Weapons seizures in Zubin Potok (Kosovo):** two seizures in two days; Bosnia: 17 arrested in Banja Luka (Operation Demidž); Montenegro: six arrested in raids on Kavač and Škaljari associates.

**Alta banka licence revocation** (RS-ALTA-BANKA) — Stage at end of week: detention. OCG: suspected (signature). Corruption: yes.
- What is reported: The National Bank of Serbia revoked Alta banka's licence on 31 Oct; the bank is owned by Davor Macura, one of four Serbian businessmen for whom US sanctions were requested. TOK checked possible money outflows abroad, then detained Macura and two treasury staff for 48 hours on suspicion of taking about 45 billion dinars out of the treasury. He denied wrongdoing; prosecutors seek up to 30 days' detention (decision not reported). Parts of the prosecution document published by KRIK and Nova allege about EUR 390m withdrawn, EUR 370m of it recorded as fictitious daily takings. North Macedonia imposed temporary administration on Alta banka Bitola (about EUR 2m reportedly withdrawn on Saturday) and Montenegro revoked the licence of Alta Pay Group Podgorica. All as reported; no verdict.
- Timeline: 2026-10-31 — licence revoked; TOK checks outflows since 27 Sep. 2026-11-01 — bank searched; North Macedonia and Montenegro act against units. 2026-11-02 — Macura detained 48h; North Macedonia temporary administration, 10,000-denar weekly withdrawal limit. 2026-11-03 — Macura questioned by JTOK; detention sought. 2026-11-04 — prosecution document published, Jorgovanka Tabaković listed as witness.
- People: Davor Macura (businessman, bank owner, detained, suspected); Jorgovanka Tabaković (former central bank governor, witness).
- Organisations: Alta banka (bank). Institutions acting: NBS; TOK/JTOK.
- Amounts: about 45 billion dinars (about EUR 383m); about EUR 390m (EUR 370m as fictitious takings); about EUR 2m withdrawn from Alta banka Bitola.
